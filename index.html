Voltage Judge Settings

Place the iPad 2–4 metres away in portrait or landscape, facing the athlete. Keep the full body and RAM visible, including hands overhead. Stand tall briefly for front-view calibration. Keep the RAM and both arms in frame. Bright, even lighting improves tracking.

Important: This is an experimental training aid, not official TRYKA judging software. Front-facing squat depth is a rough 2D estimate, not an official depth measurement. Side view is preferable when depth accuracy matters. Lockout is also estimated. A no-rep counter only catches some invalid attempts; it cannot reliably identify all faults.
